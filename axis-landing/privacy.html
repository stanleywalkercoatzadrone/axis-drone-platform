We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, use our services, or communicate with us, including:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

We retain operational data for the duration of your active subscription plus 90 days after account closure, unless a longer retention period is required by law or you request earlier deletion.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@coatzadrone.com.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and platform preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.
